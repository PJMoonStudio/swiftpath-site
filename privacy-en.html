Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SwiftPath (the “App”) handles information when you use the App.

1. Overview

SwiftPath is designed to work primarily on your device. The App does not require you to create a SwiftPath account.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide learning and review features, SwiftPath may store information locally on your device, including:

  • learning progress and completion status;
  • bookmarks;
  • quiz sessions, answers, results, and recent history;
  • weak-point and review-related data derived from your quiz activity;
  • app preferences and settings.

This information is used to provide features such as learning progress, quizzes, daily review, and weak-point review. SwiftPath does not send these learning records to a SwiftPath-operated server.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. SwiftPath may receive limited transaction or entitlement information from Apple that is necessary to determine whether Pro features are available to you.

SwiftPath does not receive your payment card number or other full payment details. Apple handles payment information under Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

4. Contacting us

If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any details you include in your request. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry, provide support, or address issues you report.

5. External links and third-party services

SwiftPath may open external pages or services, including App Store subscription management, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, or support pages. When you use an external service, that service may process information under its own privacy policy.

For example, legal and support pages may be hosted through GitHub Pages, and purchases or subscription management are handled by Apple. When you visit a page hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and service operation purposes in accordance with GitHub’s own policies.

6. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

SwiftPath does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Apple may independently collect App Store, device, diagnostic, or crash-related information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s own policies. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by SwiftPath.

7. Data sharing and sale

SwiftPath does not sell your personal information. SwiftPath does not share your locally stored learning data with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect legal rights, or respond to a valid legal request.

8. Data retention and deletion

Learning data stored locally on your device remains there until you remove or reset it through available App features, or uninstall the App. Whether app data is included in or restored from backups depends on iOS backup and restore behavior.

If you contact us directly, support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, or comply with legal obligations.

9. Children’s privacy

SwiftPath is a general-purpose programming learning app and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly use the App to collect personal information from children through a SwiftPath-operated account or server.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to design SwiftPath so that learning data remains on your device and only the information needed for App Store purchases and external services is handled by the relevant provider. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International use

You may use SwiftPath from different countries or regions. Third-party providers such as Apple or GitHub may process information in accordance with their own infrastructure, terms, and privacy policies.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to SwiftPath, legal requirements, or how information is handled. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like to request deletion of information you previously provided to us through support, contact us at pjmoonstudio@gmail.com.

Summary: SwiftPath is designed to keep learning progress, quiz history, bookmarks, and weak-point data on your device. App Store purchases are handled by Apple, and SwiftPath does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking.
